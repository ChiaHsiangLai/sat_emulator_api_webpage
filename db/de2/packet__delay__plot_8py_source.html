1 """
2 packet_delay_plot.py — plots UE -> NCC packet delay over time.
3 
4 Each input file is one of:
5  - log_file/NCC/ncc_e2e_delay_ut<N> (written by the native ./NCC process):
6  one line per uplink packet, "<unix_timestamp> <delay_seconds>", where
7  delay_seconds is the TRUE end-to-end delay from when the UT tagged the
8  packet with its send time (SendTimeTag, set in UtMAC::EncapTcPkt) to
9  the moment NCC deserialized that same packet in NccMac::ReceiveTcPkt.
10  This covers MAC queueing + TBTP wait + transmission + propagation, i.e.
11  the whole UE-to-NCC path.
12  - log_file/UT_10.0.1.<N>/ut_queueing_delay_ue<N> (written by the native
13  ./UT process): one line per uplink packet, "<unix_timestamp>
14  <delay_seconds>", where delay_seconds is only the UT-side MAC queueing
15  component (time spent waiting for a TBTP transmit opportunity) —
16  kept as a fallback/comparison source, NOT the full end-to-end delay.
17 
18 Usage:
19  packet_delay_plot.py <start_time> <output_file> <file1> [<file2> ...] [--zero_start]
20  e.g. packet_delay_plot.py 1784790352.0 ut101/ut101_packet_delay.png \\
21  log_file/NCC/ncc_e2e_delay_ut101 --zero_start
22 """
